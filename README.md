# Beyond Output Quality

**Structural Guarantees, Authority, and Auditability in ParliamentRAG** — a research response to Tritella, Pozzi & Palmonari, *Who Speaks Matters: Authority-Aware Multi-View RAG over Italian Parliamentary Proceedings* (arXiv:2608.13410).

The response argues that ParliamentRAG's most consequential property is not its scores against a monolithic baseline but what its architecture makes it possible to **know** about a generated answer — and proposes a family of evaluation questions for systems whose normative choices have moved out of prompts and into architecture: traceability, failure localization, policy propagation, historical reconstruction, and the survival of disagreement through integration.
